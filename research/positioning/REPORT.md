# Positioning across the complex: what the crowd state paid

Built by `research/positioning/complex.py` into `complex_evidence.json` (2026-10-30). Served live by `GET /api/complex?market=ID`, drawn in the Crowd map panel under Levels, and quoted in the Desk gate list and positioning facts when the live state matches a tested state.

Method. CFTC legacy futures-only, trailing 156 reports, mid-rank percentiles, point in time (the house method and the TradingView indicator's number). Entry at the close on the Friday after the Tuesday observation; forward 4, 8 and 13 week simple returns, 2012 to 2026 (Russell from 2017, Bitcoin from 2018, Ether from 2021). Crowded long: managers at or above the 90th percentile with commercials at or below the 10th on the observation. Crowded short: managers at or below the 10th with commercials at or above the 90th. Relative test: within a complex, weeks where A's managers percentile exceeded B's by 50 points or more; the statistic is B's forward return minus A's, against the pair's unconditional spread (which is zero by construction). Forward windows overlap, so n counts weeks and "independent" keeps every eighth qualifying week. No costs. History, not probability.

## What did not work

1. **The relative test is weak as a signal.** Pooled over all US index pairs, when one index's managers sat 50 or more points above another's, the less crowded index beat the more crowded by only +0.27% over 13 weeks, ahead in 56% of 727 overlapping weeks (about 96 independent). FX pairs +0.53% (56%), metals +0.07%, Treasuries +0.02%. Energy ran the other way (−1.95%) and softs ran strongly the other way (−4.63%, less crowded ahead in only 39% of 1,351 weeks): in softs the crowded market kept leading. Grains (+2.52%, n 357) and livestock (+4.93%, n 297) are positive but thin at 48 and 40 independent weeks. The map is a location, not a ranking signal.
2. **Single equity pairs disagree with each other.** RTY over NQ when NQ's managers were 50 points higher: +2.8% (71%, n 42, about 6 independent). NQ over RTY in the mirror case: −0.4% (55%, n 55). NQ over YM +2.3% (67%, n 63) but YM over NQ +0.3% (48%). With these counts the +2.8% is consistent with chance; the panel shows each pair with its n and a thin flag below 10 independent weeks and leads with the pooled number.
3. **The same-side haircut is not universal.** It is real in the indices other than the Dow (ES +1.4% against +3.2% all weeks, NQ +1.6% against +4.6%, RTY −2.2% against +2.4%), in silver, platinum, palladium, crude, natural gas, products, wheat, cattle, hogs, Treasuries and Bitcoin. The opposite held in gold (+4.3% against +1.9%), copper (+4.9% against +1.4%), soybeans (+5.7% against +0.6%), corn, all four softs (cocoa +6.5% against +3.4%, cotton +3.7% against +0.5%, sugar +2.7% against +0.3%, coffee +3.8% against +2.0%), feeder cattle and the Swiss franc: there the crowd persisted. A universal "crowded means fade" rule would have been wrong in a third of the book.
4. **Fading a crowded short did not pay everywhere either.** Longs against a crowded short lost in natural gas (−8.2% against +3.4% for all weeks, 31% positive, n 109), RBOB (−4.1%), soybeans (−2.8%), corn (−2.1%), cocoa, sugar and the Pound. They paid in NQ (+6.5% against +4.6%, 86% positive), the Dow (+5.7%), silver (+5.9% against +2.2%), crude (+6.9%), the Kiwi, the franc and feeder cattle. Bitcoin's +61% (n 44, 6 independent) is a handful of 2018 to 2020 episodes.
5. **Coffee's crowd fade shows no lift on this crude test.** Longs with the managers at or below the 10th and commercials at or above the 90th returned +1.2% over 13 weeks against +2.0% for all weeks (45% against 48% positive, n 157). The Desk's Crowd fade archetype adds valuation agreement, a not-yet-turned window and a confirmation state, and the Does-it-pay panel's replay for that cell is the better matched evidence; this test only says the raw extreme carries no payoff of its own in coffee.
6. **Counts are thinner than they look.** Every crowded episode lasts weeks, so 80 qualifying weeks in NQ are about 11 independent episodes. Treat anything under 10 independent as colour.

## What held up

- **Index crowd haircut.** Pooled ES, NQ, YM, RTY: longs returned +3.3% over 13 weeks across all weeks (72% positive, 2,728 weeks) against +1.8% (67%, 271 weeks) when the managers were crowded long. In NQ the positive rate fell from 76% to 61%. This is the measured version of the old "size warning only for an index" line, and it now carries the numbers: the gate reads "crowd on the same side: in this market longs taken with the managers at the extreme returned +1.6% over 13 weeks against +4.6% for all weeks (61% vs 76% positive, 80 weeks, about 11 independent). Size down."
- **Index longs against a crowded short.** Pooled, shorts taken with the crowd lost 3.9% over 13 weeks (30% positive, 262 weeks), so longs against them made +3.9%. NQ +6.5% (86%), YM +5.7% (81%); ES only +0.2% and RTY +0.8%, so this is mostly a Dow and NASDAQ result.
- **Silver.** Longs against a crowded short +5.9% (68%) against +2.2% (50%) for all weeks, 96 weeks, about 13 independent. Gold shows the same direction with a smaller gap (+2.1% against +1.9%, 67% against 58% positive).
- **Direction of the haircut is market specific and stable in sign at 8 and 13 weeks** for the markets with 10 or more independent episodes, which is why the live wording is per market and never pooled across the book.

## How it is used in the product

- `bhws_core/crowd.py` loads the evidence once and classifies the live observation with the same thresholds. When the Desk side is long and the live state is crowded long (or short and crowded short) the gate list carries the measured sentence in place of the generic index line, and only when the measured lift is negative; persistence markets get the fact ("No measured penalty: the crowd has tended to persist in this market") but no size gate. When the trade fades the crowd the positioning facts carry the measured fade sentence with its verdict (a measured lift, no measured difference, a measured drag). Markets without a research row keep the old wording; nothing is replaced silently.
- `GET /api/complex?market=ID` returns the complex, every peer's current percentiles with its Desk class and side, the market's crowd history, the pooled complex numbers, the relative pairs involving the market, server-written read sentences and the definitions above.
- The Crowd map panel shows the peers on a 0 to 100 strip (commercials blue, managers gold, retail red, extreme bands at 10 and 90), sorted by managers with the current market highlighted, then the crowd history for the Desk side with a Longs/Shorts toggle, the pooled complex line and the pairs with thin flags, then the caveat.

## What this does not say

No costs, no slippage, no stop; a 13-week simple return is not a trade. Percentile thresholds at 90 and 10 are the research cut, not a claim that 89 is different from 90. Nothing here is a probability for the trade in front of you; it is the history of this market in this state, shown next to the live state so you can weigh it.
